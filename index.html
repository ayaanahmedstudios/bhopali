Where it comes from

Bhopali takes its name from Bhopal, the city in Central India where its designer, Ayaan Ahmed, was born. The letterforms borrow from the city's architecture — the smooth curve of a dome, the sharp edge of an archway, the quiet solidity of old stonework.

It's a display face first: drawn to be set big, at headline size, where its rhythm of thick strokes and calm serifs can actually be felt. It isn't trying to disappear into a paragraph — it wants the room.
